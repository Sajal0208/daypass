# Code of conduct

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Assume good faith; ask before accusing.
- Keep reviews about the code and its safety, and make them actionable.

If someone behaves badly, email **conduct@vocobase.com**. Reports are handled privately by the
maintainers, who may warn, remove content, or ban as needed.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
